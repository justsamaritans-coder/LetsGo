A trip plan that sounds like you

Answer a few questions about how you like to travel. A person and an AI plan your trip with you, one slot at a time, and you decide what goes in.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the trip. Dates, who is going, where from. About 8 minutes.
  2. Choose where. Know the place? We check the fit. Still deciding? You get three ranked picks.
  3. Build day by day. For each slot you see one pick and two alternatives.
  4. Rate the plan. One question: did it feel like you?
